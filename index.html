Used tea leaves

Let leaves dry, then mix directly into potting soil or the base of plants.

Adds nitrogen; improves soil texture
🥚

Crushed eggshells

Dry, crush into powder, sprinkle around the base of plants.

Rich in calcium; deters some soft-bodied pests
🍌

Banana peels

Chop and bury a few inches deep near the root zone, or soak in water 2–3 days for a liquid feed.

High in potassium and phosphorus
🥔

Vegetable peels & scraps

Compost in a pit or bin with dry leaves; turn every week.

Builds organic compost rich in micronutrients
☕

Coffee grounds

Sprinkle dried grounds lightly around acid-loving plants or add to compost.

Adds nitrogen; improves soil aeration
🍚

Rice rinse water

Save the starchy water from washing rice and water plants with it directly.

Mild nutrient boost; encourages beneficial soil microbes
🔥

Wood ash

Cool completely, then dust a thin layer over soil, away from seedlings.

Adds potassium; raises soil pH slightly
🍂

Dry leaves (leaf mould)

Pile and let them decompose for 2–3 months, turning occasionally.

Improves soil structure and water retention
